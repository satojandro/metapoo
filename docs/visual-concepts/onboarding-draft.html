Onboarding mockup · PRIVY-1 + INTENTS-1

From “never heard of NEAR” to paint on the wall.

A new player signs in with Google or email, gets a NEAR account they never had to set up, tops it up from whatever chain their money is on, hands the browser a drawing key, and paints. Seven screens, one sheet, no new page.

Every quote, address, route and balance below is a sample. Board figures (0.01 NEAR to register, 0.0025 NEAR a tile, 25 free tiles, 0.25 NEAR key allowance) are mainnet values from 9 Nov; the app reads them live. Tags: BUILT exists behind a flag (PR #56) · NEW UI needs design work · GATE blocked on live evidence · DECIDE owner call.

Owner agreed 9 Nov · does Intents need a new screen?

No new route. A step in the same sheet.

“Add funds” is the third step of the sign-up sheet that slides up over the wall, the same pattern as Buy credits. The wall stays mounted, so the player’s draft and place survive a 5-minute wait for a cross-chain deposit. The same sheet opens from the wallet menu (“Add funds”) for players who are already in.

A separate /add-funds page would break the spec’s rule that funding never unmounts the wall, and an empty account would have to bounce between pages. The one exception worth a link is /agents → “No NEAR yet?”, which opens this sheet (see open decision 3).

The order is fixed by the specs

Paint→Sign in · Privy→Account address→Add funds · Intents→Verified balance→Drawing key→Paint
  • The key is not requested at sign-in. The new account doesn’t exist on chain yet and can’t pay for AddKey.
  • Funding never buys credits. Signing in never adds a key. Each is its own explicit tap.
  • Players pay their own gas. No sponsor, no relayer.
  • Existing NEAR wallets stay one tap away on the very first sheet.

GATE · INT-NATIVE

Nobody has yet shown that a 1Click delivery to a fresh, uninitialized implicit account (exactly what a new Privy player has) arrives as native NEAR and creates the account. Until a recorded trial proves it, the spec blocks the Intents route for empty accounts and offers the direct NEAR receive address instead. This is the screen that ships first.

Once the gate passes with deliveryMode=native, screen 3 becomes the version above, and Intents is the very first thing a social-login player sees.

This is also why INTENTS-ROUTES “pairs with Privy” in PR #92: social login is only complete end to end for people without NEAR once this gate is closed.

When it goes sideways

What the sheet says.

Popup blocked or closed

The sign-in window didn’t open. Try again

Blocked and closed are separate states, and neither auto-retries. Cancelling sign-in is not a failed purchase.

Quote expired, nothing sent

This payment address has expired. Don’t send to it. Get a new quote

Send instructions hide at the expiry margin. The record and status check stay.

Taking longer than usual

Still checking. Cross-chain transfers can take up to 15 minutes.

A timeout is never shown as “Failed”.

Refund on its way

The conversion didn’t go through. Your USDC is being returned to 0x71C9…3b8E.

INCOMPLETE_DEPOSIT and FAILED are never shown as “refunded” until there is evidence.

Signed in as someone else now

This payment is for 8f3c…a91e. Switch back to that account to continue.

A deposit is never retargeted to the new account; read-only tracking continues.

Arrived, but short

0.12 NEAR arrived. You need 0.30 NEAR to start; send 0.18 more.

Shows the real balance and the shortfall, never “ready”.

Coming back, same browser

Welcome back. Your drawing key is still live.

Painting doesn’t need a fresh Privy sign-in; spending money does.

New phone or browser

Same account, new browser. Set up a drawing key here to paint.

Drawing secrets are never copied between devices.

Open decisions.

None of these blocks turning the flags on for testing. Each has a default below that is safe for testnet and the first trial; testing produces the evidence to settle it.
