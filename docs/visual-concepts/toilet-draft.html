TOILET

Hold the wall. Get paid in TOILET.

Every tile you paint with credits earns TOILET for as long as it survives. Paint over someone else’s tile and it starts earning for you instead. Stake your TOILET and you get 70% of the mPOO the stall takes in.

Decided 2 Nov · specced A fixed rate per tile, with price-style guards

Emission stays a fixed rate per earning tile per second, as the code does it. It is not keyed to revenue. A tile earns the same on an empty wall as on a full one, and the billion lasts longer while the wall fills.

Today toilet_per_cell_second can move any amount at any time through set_tunables. The TOILET-round spec moves it to its own setter with the same guards as prices: at most ×2 or ÷2 per change, a 24-hour cooldown and a public event. Dropping it to 0 stays instant, as an emergency stop.

Whales welcome

Buy the wall if you like. The internet will make you pay to keep it.

There’s no earning cap. Paint a thousand tiles and all of them earn. But every tile is up for grabs, and holding one means answering every poke while the poker’s paint is still wet. Every fight sells credits, and every credit sold feeds the stakers.

Decided 2 Nov · specced No earning cap · free tiles earn nothing

The earning cap is dropped and max_earning_cells will be deleted, so get_tunables stops advertising a limit nobody enforces (audit C3). The whale risk is accepted on purpose: the defence is the community, and the fight is revenue.

Free tiles (first paints, happy hour) earn nothing until someone repaints them with credits. Nothing has to be burned for them. TOILET is only set aside against the cap when an earning tile accrues it, so a free tile’s share is simply never minted and stays in the unissued part of the billion.

Staking

Stake TOILET. Collect the stall’s mPOO.

Painters pay NEAR and mPOO. The stall buys mPOO with the NEAR and sends 70% of all its mPOO to the stakers’ pool. The pool pays out at most 10% of itself a day, pro rata to stake, so payouts arrive as a steady drip and the pool swells when the wall is busy.

Specced 2 Nov · not built Staking contract

The spec covers staking TOILET, a pool that releases at most 10% of itself per day, pro rata mPOO rewards, and a 10% exit tax, burned, on each unstaked amount. The board stays as it is.

The drip smooths payouts. It doesn’t grow them: at steady state the pool holds about ten days of inflow and pays out what comes in. Two things the contract has to get right because the board won’t change. The board pays the pool with a plain ft_transfer, so the contract has to count new mPOO from its own balance. And set_staking_contract works once, ever, so the contract gets a review gate before that call.

Supply

One billion. Then the tap’s off.

TOILET is only minted when a holder claims what their tiles earned. There’s no owner, no admin mint and no upgrade path. When the billion is reserved, earning stops for good.

Hard cap

1,000,000,000

TOILET earned is set aside against the cap the moment it accrues, so a late claimer never finds the cupboard bare.

Decided 2 Nov TOILET is for staking, not for paint

TOILET won’t be spendable on paint. The decisions doc still says it is “spent/burned as paint”, and the TOILET-round PR corrects that. Its uses are staking, trading and burning.

The exchange tax is a nice-to-have: match mPOO’s 2% buy and 3% sell. The rates and the burn/treasury split are fixed forever at deploy (D4), so they’re chosen before deploy, with a test on a real pool first.
