How it works

The only public stall on the whole internet.

One wall, 128 × 128 tiles, open to everyone on earth. Write your name, draw something rude, leave a love note, cross out somebody else’s. Come back tomorrow and see what survived.

Rules of the stall

  1. Leave your mark. Every tile you paint stays until someone paints over it.
  2. Wet paint costs more to cover. Fresh work is protected for its first hour.
  3. Your first 25 tiles are free while the wall fills up.
  4. The stall runs itself. Nobody pockets what it earns.
  5. Wash your hands.

Painting · try it

Wet paint costs more to cover.

You paint with credits. A dry or empty tile costs 1. Paint from the last hour costs 2 to cover, and paint from the last 10 minutes costs 4. New work gets a moment on the wall before someone wipes it.

The autonomous stall

The stall runs itself.

Once you buy credits, that money belongs to the game. It doesn’t sit in anyone’s wallet. The contract holds it and sends it out on a schedule, along lines nobody can redraw. Here’s what the stall does with what it earns.

1 · It earns

Painters buy credits

NEAR and mPOO land on the board. 10% of the NEAR goes to the team.

2 · It buys mPOO

90% of NEAR buys back

The buyback swaps NEAR for mPOO on the market and hands it back to the stall.

3 · It pays stakers

70% of mPOO

Goes to people staking TOILET. 10% goes to the team.

4 · It flushes

20% of mPOO

Sent to an address with no key. It never comes back.

House rules, enforced by code

Not even the owner can rig the stall.

The owner can tune a few knobs. The contract decides how far and how fast, and every change is a public transaction.

No price shocks

Prices can at most double or halve per change, once every 24 hours. Repaint prices count too.

Wet paint stays protected

Covering a tile tops out at 8 credits, and fresher paint always costs at least as much as dry.

No surprise happy hours

Ending one is instant. Starting, moving or growing one waits 24 hours.

The split is welded on

10 / 90 for NEAR, 10 / 70 / 20 for mPOO. Changing it takes a new contract and a review.

Payout accounts move slowly

Team and buyback accounts can change once a week, in public. The staking contract is set once, ever.

One emergency stop

The owner can pause painting and buying. The wall stays readable and payouts keep flowing.
