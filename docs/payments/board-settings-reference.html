Each setting the owner can change on the paid board, what it does, its launch value, its limits and how fast it can change. Values are the code's defaults and the decided launch values, not live readings: the admin dashboard will read them from the chain.

Open decisions

  • Distribution interval has no limit. The owner could set it to years and freeze revenue sharing. Proposed: bound it to 10 minutes – 24 hours.
  • Pause and distributions. Today pausing stops painting and buying, but distributions keep running. Stop them too?
  • mPOO daily cap at launch. Recommended 20,000 credits a day.
  • Testnet team and buyback accounts. Needed for the rehearsal.

How a change is made today

  • Prices: set_near_price, set_mpoo_price.
  • Emergency stop: set_paused.
  • Revenue accounts: set_team_account, set_buyback_account, set_staking_contract.
  • Everything else: set_tunables, which replaces the whole settings record. Read the latest record, change one field, send it all back. History fields must be sent unchanged or the call fails.
  • All of these are owner-only and need 1 yoctoNEAR attached. The dashboard will build these calls for you.
